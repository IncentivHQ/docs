# Incentiv Bridge

The **Incentiv Bridge** lets you move supported assets between Incentiv and connected networks through an interface operated by Incentiv.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Open Incentiv Bridge" color="#FE7233" icon="bridge" href="https://bridge.incentiv.io/">
    Transfer assets to or from Incentiv.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Bridge Explorer" color="#FE7233" icon="magnifying-glass" href="https://explorer.bridge.incentiv.io/">
    Track the status of a bridge transfer.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  Always use the official URLs shown above. Check the selected token, source network, destination network, recipient address, and estimated fees before confirming a transfer.
</Warning>

</Note>

## Bridge an asset

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the bridge">
    Go to [bridge.incentiv.io](https://bridge.incentiv.io/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the asset and source network">
    Choose the token you want to transfer and the network where you currently hold it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your wallet">
    Connect the wallet that holds the asset on the source network.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the destination">
    Select the destination network. Either the source or destination must be **Incentiv**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the transfer details">
    Enter the amount to bridge. To send the asset to a different wallet, enable **Receive to Another Address** and carefully verify the recipient address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    Review the route, amount, recipient, estimated completion time, and fees. Select **Bridge**, then approve the transaction in your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track the transfer">
    Follow the transfer in the [Incentiv Bridge Explorer](https://explorer.bridge.incentiv.io/) until it is complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Settlement times

Transfer time is dominated by how long the bridge waits for the **source** chain to be safe from reorganization, plus delivery time on the destination. That means the direction of travel matters far more than the amount or the asset.

### Into Incentiv

| From | Typical | Why |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Ethereum | **3–4 minutes** | Ethereum's 15-block reorg wait (~3 min) dominates |
| Base | **~30 seconds** | 10-block wait at ~2 s blocks |
| Optimism | **~30 seconds** | 10-block wait at ~2 s blocks |
| Polygon | **under 30 seconds** | Waits for Polygon finality |
| Arbitrum | **under 15 seconds** | 5-block wait at ~0.25 s blocks |
| Solana | **~30 seconds** | No reorg wait; relayer time only |

### Out of Incentiv

| To | Typical |
| :-- | :-- |
| Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, Optimism, Polygon, Solana | **1–2 minutes** |

Outbound time is effectively the same regardless of destination: it is set by Incentiv's own 10-block (~53 second) confirmation wait plus delivery overhead, not by the receiving chain.

### What to expect

- **Most transfers settle well inside the times above.** Measured across recent live transfers, outbound delivery had a median of ~76 seconds and a 90th percentile of ~98 seconds.
- **Occasionally a transfer takes much longer.** During relayer incidents, delays of up to a few hours have been observed. These transfers still complete — funds are not lost, and the message stays queued for delivery.
- **Do not resubmit a pending transfer.** A delayed transfer will settle on its own. Sending again creates a second, independent transfer.

Check any transfer's status in the [Incentiv Bridge Explorer](https://explorer.bridge.incentiv.io/).

## Before you confirm

- Keep enough of the source network's native token to pay the source transaction fee.
- Confirm that the destination network supports the selected asset.
- Check the recipient address if you are sending to another wallet.
- Review the amount and fee estimate shown in the bridge.
- Avoid closing the wallet confirmation before the source transaction is submitted.

## Transfer status and support

Use the [Incentiv Bridge Explorer](https://explorer.bridge.incentiv.io/) to check a transfer's status.

If a transfer remains pending longer than expected, keep the source transaction hash and bridge transfer identifier available when contacting Incentiv support. Do not submit the same transfer again until you have confirmed the status of the original transfer.
